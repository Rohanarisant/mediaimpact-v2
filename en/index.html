What is the difference between classic SEO and AIO?+
Classic SEO optimises your visibility in search engine results. AIO (Artificial Intelligence Optimization) goes further: it ensures your brand is correctly represented in the responses generated by AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity — which increasingly shape how information is accessed.
How can you protect an executive's reputation against AI hallucinations?+
A proactive strategy is required: developing structured and credible content, strengthening semantic authority and continuously monitoring what AI systems generate. When false information appears, we have protocols to correct it at source.
Why is territorial strategy a key issue in Switzerland?+
The Swiss market is fragmented across multiple languages and cultures. An effective strategy in French-speaking Switzerland does not automatically work in German-speaking Switzerland. Media Impact masters both markets with adapted editorial angles and dedicated media networks.
How does compliance influence crisis communication?+
Compliance imposes a strict framework: every public statement must be legally validated and aligned with sector-specific regulatory requirements. Media Impact works closely with legal teams to ensure every message is both strategically effective and legally sound.
