Our method

Crisis Control
Framework™

The Crisis Control Framework™ is Media Impact's proprietary crisis management methodology. Built from decades of field experience, it structures intervention into four sequential and interdependent phases.

Each phase can be activated independently depending on when we enter the relationship — before, during or after a crisis. The method is adapted to Swiss and international contexts, with particular attention to compliance and territorial issues.

01
Anticipate

Identify and model risks

Reduce uncertainty before it becomes public. Rigorous prevention work is always less costly than reactive crisis management.

Compliance audit and media exposure assessment
Mapping of critical stakeholders
Identification of high-impact scenarios
SEO/AI radar pack for digital reputation engineering
02
Structure

Build the line of defence

Have an immediately deployable framework. Every hour lost at the start of a crisis costs weeks of rebuilding.

Development of key messages and response lines
Creation of a crisis cell / task force
Spokesperson preparation (media training)
Implementation of management protocols
03
Control

Manage communication under pressure

Regain narrative control in real time. Speed of response and message consistency are the two decisive factors.

Activation of the crisis cell
Management of urgent media interactions
Close coordination with legal teams
Deployment of AI campaigns where necessary
04
Stabilize

Restore and strengthen reputation

Turn a critical situation into a credibility asset. A well-managed crisis can lastingly reinforce trust in an organisation.

Rebalancing of public discourse
Reduction of negative share of voice
Strategic repositioning of executives
Continuous monitoring and adjustment
